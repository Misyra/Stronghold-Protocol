### 30.1 Fixed gifts and already displayed operator offers

Reports #488/#491/#495, PR #496 by @2321Robin, and the owner's Muelsyse/Yela report: the shared equipment
cap added in 0.2.4 incorrectly prevented fixed acquisition gifts. [路标档案馆：路标月报#2](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1eLXXBqEgF/)
states both that equipment from any source occupies stock and that these operators' fixed gifts are unlimited.
The owner confirmed on 2026-10-11: gifts still occupy stock, but exhaustion must never prevent the gift.

`GameData.isUnlimitedGiftItem` derives the families from `SERVER_GAIN_EQUIP`. The actual gift passes
`requirePool:false` through `effectsMeta` to `acquireItem`; only those families bypass availability. Ordinary and
advanced copies still count as 1/2. This covers Carnelian, Beeswax, Muelsyse, Kjera and Swire, and the same fixed
family awarded by the Damazti strategy. It does not make every strategy's equipment unlimited. Shop and item
reward purchases, random grants and 整备 upgrades keep their availability checks. When ownership exceeds the cap,
removing one copy need not immediately reopen stock. The proposed PR's zero-occupancy model was not adopted.

#498: the same video's approximately 2:50–2:57 example describes nine owned 溯光星源 plus one
already displayed card in each of four shops, allowing thirteen owned copies. Shop operator purchases therefore
do not recheck availability; `acquireChess` records the full deficit and later rolls still exclude exhausted stock.
The owner explicitly extended this behavior to already displayed **operator reward** offers; that extension is a
maintainer decision, not footage directly establishing every reward type. Item purchases/rewards remain bounded.
Funds, full-hand checks, per-offer sold flags, merge accounting and selling returns retain their ordinary rules.

Tests: `test/match/community-round30.test.js`, `shared-stock.test.js`, `garrisons_meta.test.js`.
Normative rules: META shared inventory and helper contract; design/match economy.

### 30.17 Funnel normal attacks keep a body hit

PR #537 by @Xinglan233. [PRTS 分支特性信息 驭械术师](https://prts.wiki/w/分支特性信息/data) says a drone that has not
been released by a skill deals additional damage on top of the operator's attack: 20 % ATK at first, +15 on the same
target, cap 110 %. A skill-released drone deals skill damage.

The profession used to return that ramp as the whole normal-attack multiplier, so the body hit was missing.
`resolveHit` now keeps a 100 % ATK body instance and separate drone instances. `funnelMul` holds only the drone ramp;
an attack's `dmgMul` (至简's talent) is applied once to both. Extra attached drones raise `hits`, not the body count.
至简's charged S2 sets `funnelBodyHits`, so two body hits and two additions share one ramp step. Both share one
`attackId` and one attack hook; only the body is `isAttack`. Additions carry `addition` and `funnel` and are not also
emitted as a normal attack.

[ASSUMED] Body instances precede additions at the existing projectile impact, and a multi-hit volley takes one ramp
step. This covers ordinary attacks and unreleased skill attacks that set `funnelBodyHits`. Released skill paths that
do not set it (耶拉 S2, 澄闪 S1 while its own drones are out) keep their existing skill-damage path and do not gain a
second body. Whether those released skills should also have a body attack is not settled here. 澄闪 S3 and the
skill-drone simulator on 淬羽 stay on their own `dealDamage` paths.

Kits that stored the ramp on `dmgMul` (至简, both 耶拉 forms, 澄闪 S2) now store it on `funnelMul`.

Tests: `test/sim/funnel-body.test.js`, and the funnel cases in `kits_t3`, `kits_t6`, `kits_alt_t6`, `kits_alt_t2`
and `op_gdglow`.

### 30.20 Hit tiles use half-even rounding

PR #520 by @Cloudnyco. PRTS 作战机制 (oldid 433935, 「碰撞体积与位置识别」) says the struck unit's coordinates are
rounded half to even (四舍六入五成双) onto the tile they occupy. `Math.round` sends an exact .5 toward +∞, so an enemy
held on an outer half-tile was indexed off the board and dropped from the hit index.

`roundHalfEven` is used for hit tiles only: `body.js` `posKey`, the point cases of `bodyOnTile` and `bodyTileReach`,
the point-enemy index in `battle/queries.js`, and `targeting.js` `tileKeyOf`. Movement, knockback, `ai.js`, a unit's
standing tile for range keys, and `onFrontLine` still use `Math.round`. #519's signed-zero case in `powi` was already
on this candidate and is left as it is.

[ASSUMED] `FIELD_EDGE_INSET` = 1/1048576 pulls a clamped enemy just inside the outer half-tile, so half-even rounding
keeps it on the edge tile. An inset of 1/1024 was rejected because it moved unbalance steps. The inset has no
official source.

Tests: `test/sim/hit-tile-round.test.js`, `test/sim/detmath.test.js`, the edge landing in

### 30.21 Bots soft-reserve a living teammate's opening bonds

PR #518 by @IceCodeNew, for issue #514. This is not an official rule. It extends the accepted #407 card penalty.

Opening-band choice and focus planning used to ignore a living teammate's selected strategy until someone from it was
deployed, so a bot could take that faction's high cards. A living teammate's strategy now soft-reserves its bonds for
both choices, before any member is deployed. The reservation ends when that teammate is eliminated (`alivePlayers`).
It does not hard-exclude a strategy, reserve stock, or stop an immediate merge. A reserved band's draw weight is a
quarter of its unreserved weight (`SOFT_BAND_RESERVE` = 0.25) and stays above zero, so the strategy can still be
drawn. Focus planning applies the existing −6 teammate penalty to those bonds as well. Mode-inactive bonds stay
weight 0 (DESIGN §21.26). A solo match has no teammate to reserve against. The shared-pool −18 card penalty is
unchanged, and a stronger merge can still win it.

[ASSUMED] The quarter weight is a project choice. The PR text called the reservation soft; its diff hard-zeroed the
weight. This candidate follows the text. The reservation is equal for human and AI teammates.

Tests: the two `#514` cases in `test/match/bot-smarter.test.js`. One proves the focus leaves the reserved bond while
the teammate lives and returns after elimination. The other proves a reserved strategy is still drawn, and drawn more
often once that teammate is eliminated. The existing card test still shows a merge outscoring the −18 penalty.
