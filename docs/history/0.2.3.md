## §28.12 浏览器验收退出时释放自有日志管道

完整浏览器验收中，真实对局与观战的断言已经全部通过，但 Chrome 的崩溃报告/更新辅助进程继承了日志管道，测试进程仍在等待读取结束。共用测试客户端现在在浏览器子进程明确退出后释放它的 stdio；关闭失败、子进程仍活着时不关闭管道，也不操作其他浏览器进程。游戏运行时代码与战斗规则不变。

新增真实子进程继承 stderr 的回归，以及关闭失败/信号退出的边界检查；验收要求原异常文件单独重跑并确认正常结束。首轮异常与复测结果分别保留在本轮验收记录中。

同一轮的版本重连测试在刻意停服时，Chrome 可能报告连接拒绝、连接重置或未连接的 socket。测试只在主动停服至恢复握手的窗口内排除这些已知传输日志；正常运行期间仍检查它们，脚本异常始终保留，所有版本指纹、重连及换局刷新断言不变。

### 28.24 Echo / counter recursion found by the cooperative benchmark

The 40-seed benchmark exposed existing hook-depth errors in co-op seeds 3 and 29, plus seed 14 after the purchasing
change. In all three, “余音” damage pulses and 刺玫 / 泡泡 counters synchronously called each other. A scaled echo can
have 67 hit-count HP; the generic 32-hook guard cut off effects before the legitimate chain finished. The same chain
could also enter another hit while zero HP was waiting for death bookkeeping.

[PRTS “余音”](https://prts.wiki/w/“余音”) establishes the hit-count and damage-pulse behavior. [ASSUMED] The exact nested
ordering is not documented: each echo now queues recursively earned pulses and resolves them FIFO in the same call,
after the current pulse finishes. Every earned pulse retains its attack value, including the lethal hit's pulse. No
extra tick, RNG draw, global hook-depth increase or arbitrary pulse suppression is introduced. Death prevents further
counter targeting normally. This local scheduling choice needs final fidelity review.

Tests use real elite 刺玫 and 泡泡 against 67-hit echoes: all 67 pulses resolve, the echo dies, no engine error is logged,
and the queue clears. Both tests fail against the previous implementation. Together with the existing enemy/boss and
刺玫 healing suites, 273 tests pass. The benchmark now writes actual diagnostic messages beside nonzero error counts,
so failures cannot be mistaken for ordinary performance results. Recomputed golden and final benchmark results are
recorded with the candidate; do not reuse the earlier, erroneous benchmark as a clean result.
