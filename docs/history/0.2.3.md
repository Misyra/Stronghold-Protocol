## 28. New content and community fixes

This chapter describes the development version, not a published release. Numerical content is rebuilt from

### 28.1 克莱门莎 — `sim/content/kits/ops/op-clemnt.js`

- **Source:** [PRTS 克莱门莎](https://prts.wiki/w/克莱门莎) and the official character, skill and range tables.
  The non-collaboration 6★ roster gains Clementia (`char_4231_clemnt`), a 本源近卫: 72 DIY operators,
  89 backup units including stand-ins, 259 selectable forms. All three skills are selectable in the existing
  tier-5 / tier-6 DIY slots at their existing skill-rank rules; potential and cultivation follow the player's settings.
  She has no launch module. Her bonds follow the existing faction mapping, including 阿戈尔.
- **Talents:** each physical hit independently rolls 生死定夺's pre-DEF ATK multiplier; an enemy with current
  DEF below its initial DEF uses the higher probability. 习得性防御 reduces physical / Arts damage from the
  forward half-plane (including its boundary) or an enemy she blocks; 海怪 uses its separate reduction.
  Both talents take their potential-sensitive values from the generated data.
- **S1:** the charged attack applies its ATK scale and inflicts 侵蚀损伤 proportional to actual HP damage.
  **S2:** the cabin travels forwards at 2 tiles/s, up to 3 tiles, carrying up to 15 ground enemies with a total
  weight of 8. Its collision square is 1.1 × 1.1. Impassable terrain stops it; self-bound / teleport-immune
  units cannot ride. Its actual stopping position becomes a radius-1.5 vortex that can affect air, slows by 70%, and each
  second deals 200 erosion damage plus the skill's physical damage. Erosion follows the engine's normal
  resistance, burst cooldown and mode-leader immunity rules.
  Ordinary skill attacks hit their main ground target and up to four neighbours.
- **S3:** attacks select up to three ground enemies and add erosion based on actual HP damage without spending
  ammunition. A selectable ground enemy's erosion burst anywhere in the current battle marks it for 1 s.
  The closest mark consumes one of 10 bombardments: three physical + elemental hits in the target tile's
  five-tile cross. A tile with an outstanding projectile cannot be selected again for 5 s. Values are taken
  from the skill rank, not inferred from the final rank's description.
- **[ASSUMED], pending PRTS confirmation:** S2 ordinary splash radius 1.5 and stable spawn-order tie breaks;
  放逐 presently prevents walking, attacking and blocking while keeping the passenger damageable; cabin
  movement updates every sim tick, stops 0.4 tiles before an impassable edge, and the vortex ends with S2.
  S3's first impact is 0.5 s after launch, the others 1 s apart; launched projectiles finish after skill end.
  These are local kit assumptions, not changes to displacement or targeting rules for other operators.
- **Tests:** `test/content/op_clemnt.test.js` covers available forms, potential, crit probabilities, directional
  defence, S1 cadence, S2 capacity / immunity / terrain / air vortex / target cap, and S3 ammo / global marks /
  mixed damage. Golden changes and the final integration verification are recorded below once complete.

### 28.3 Clipboard export inside dialogs — #433

Reported by @RushFTK: exporting settings over an insecure/LAN connection focused the language selector and
reported success without copying the export. The fallback textarea was appended outside the active dialog,
whose focus trap redirected selection before `execCommand('copy')`.

`ui/clipboard.js` now selects its temporary textarea inside the active dialog/guide, validates focus and the
whole selection before copying, and always removes it and restores focus, including after exceptions.
The secure Clipboard API remains first choice; a failed fallback returns false for the existing manual-copy UI.
Shared invite/loadout copying uses the same helper. Device tests passed 16/16; independent interaction probes
passed 12/12. The real nested-dialog regression is included in `test/ui/modal.e2e.test.js` for integration testing.

### 28.4 蕾缪安's wanted range also triggers skills — #428

Reported by @CurlyZhu. Marked enemies' tiles extended normal targeting, but were absent from the skill's
initial trigger range: full SP could sit idle while she attacked a remote wanted enemy. Her kit now registers
those tiles through the existing `addTriggerRange` contract, retaining target selectability and operation cooldown.
[PRTS 蕾缪安](https://prts.wiki/w/蕾缪安) also clarifies that a new detection timer needs a selectable target,
a timer already attached continues while sleeping within detection range, and the source leaving clears its
timers and marks. Each source now tracks its own progress; one Lemuen leaving does not erase another's marks.
The focused 12 tests cover all three skills, cross-player detection in a joint field, sleep/wake, multiple sources,
redeployment (including immediate range cleanup before the next attack) and the existing S3 waiting/late-bombardment behavior. Independent review passed after fixing that cleanup.

Golden: all 285 scenarios recomputed. `roster-016/018/035/036/045`, `boss-boss_1/3/5/7-pair`,
`hidden-boss_9-pair` and `solo-HARD-9-boosted` move because wanted marks now drive skill casts and
source-owned detection/cleanup; allies and kill timing consequently change. Bonds, stand-ins and DIY are unchanged.

### 28.6 Asset download visibility — PR #421

Ported from [PR #421](https://github.com/sganggs/Stronghold-Protocol/pull/421) by @LimitlessHPPK.
The asset resolver records every planned leaf with no alternative on disk as `droppedLeaves` in its report;
the terminal prints a bounded summary. `--strict` / `SP_ASSETS_STRICT=1` returns exit 1 for those omissions.
The default remains a warning, and the existing manifest shrink guard remains in force. Strict mode changes
the outcome code; it is not a transaction and does not roll back a manifest already written. Temporary test
assets are cleaned up after each case. This is an opt-in tool check, not an added packaging requirement.

### 28.8 Stop recalculating held Spine poses — PR #434

Ported from [PR #434](https://github.com/sganggs/Stronghold-Protocol/pull/434) by @Cloudnyco. A finished,
non-looping, unmixed death pose or frozen model can reuse its posed skeleton and cached pixels while its
clock advances. First poses, new clips, tint changes, mixing, multiple tracks, pending wind-ups / closing clips,
clipping switches, dirty textures and zoom rescaling still update. Redeployment resumes normal animation.
Redeploy countdown labels share rasterized textures keyed by text, colour and resolution; late web fonts refresh
them in place. The 64 targeted renderer tests passed. The author's performance measurements are not reproduced
here; real Pixi checks are part of the integration pass. No battle rule or golden result is intentionally changed.

### 28.9 Preserve facing during pushes and pulls — #418 / PR #427

Ported from [PR #427](https://github.com/sganggs/Stronghold-Protocol/pull/427) by @YinqiMargatroid.
Successful pushes/pulls mark their displacement events `keepFacing` so the renderer retains pre-hit facing
while waiting for the endpoint snapshot and throughout the slide. Raw displacement and explicit
`keepFacing: false` retain the old directional behavior. Position, distance and unbalance time are unchanged.
The contributor's source clips and their limitations are retained in research 13; those observations were not
independently replayed for this port. **[ASSUMED]** other push sources and pulls against the target's old facing
share the examples' display behavior; the drag template alone cannot establish that engine rule.
Tests cover real push/pull skill paths, default/override metadata, snapshot reversal, and an earlier attack
arriving after slide prestart in either field direction. Golden metadata deliberately omits the display flag.

### 28.10 Charged guardian heals and mixed-skill activation — #406

Reported by @ChengCC0101. 塞雷娅 / 黍 S1 previously required an enemy to supply the attack that their charged heal
replaces. [PRTS 塞雷娅](https://prts.wiki/w/塞雷娅) and [黍](https://prts.wiki/w/黍) describe an eligible ally at
half HP or below, with the normal attack cadence. `updateAlly` now checks this alternate target after its cooldown
and control/disarm checks; an enemy is unnecessary. An invalid pending heal still returns its charge. This corrects
the enemy requirement inferred in historical §27.31; that earlier conclusion is superseded.

[PRTS 凯尔希·思衡托](https://prts.wiki/w/凯尔希·思衡托) S2 can attack enemies and heal allies. Its automatic
activation now accepts either target kind on its expanded range, under the existing player-approved range strategy
and operation cooldown. S1/S3 keep their existing trigger rules. This addresses the healing/activation parts of #406;
the separate fatal-hit/silence report still needs an independently justified damage-order fix.

Verification: 104 targeted tests pass. Independent interaction review found and verified fixes for a one-frame
cadence delay and premature charge use while disarmed; normal/100-ASPD intervals are exactly 1.2/0.6 s.
Sleep, silence, no-heal targets, charge refunds, cooperative healing and S2 target selectability remain covered.

Golden: all 285 scenarios recomputed; `diy-059`, `diy-133` change because
charged heals now act without enemy targets and S2 can open for enemies. Other scenarios remain byte-identical.
