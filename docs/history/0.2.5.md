### 30.1 Fixed gifts and already displayed operator offers

Reports #488/#491/#495, PR #496 by @2321Robin, and the owner's Muelsyse/Yela report: the shared equipment
cap added in 0.2.4 incorrectly prevented fixed acquisition gifts. [路标档案馆：路标月报#2](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1eLXXBqEgF/)
states both that equipment from any source occupies stock and that these operators' fixed gifts are unlimited.
The owner confirmed on 2026-10-11: gifts still occupy stock, but exhaustion must never prevent the gift.

`GameData.isUnlimitedGiftItem` derives the families from `SERVER_GAIN_EQUIP`. The actual gift passes
`requirePool:false` through `effectsMeta` to `acquireItem`; only those families bypass availability. Ordinary and
advanced copies still count as 1/2. This covers Carnelian, Beeswax, Muelsyse, Kjera and Swire, and the same fixed
family awarded by the Damazti strategy. It does not make every strategy's equipment unlimited. Shop and item
reward purchases, random grants and 整备 upgrades keep their availability checks. When ownership exceeds the cap,
removing one copy need not immediately reopen stock. The proposed PR's zero-occupancy model was not adopted.

#498: the same video's approximately 2:50–2:57 example describes nine owned 溯光星源 plus one
already displayed card in each of four shops, allowing thirteen owned copies. Shop operator purchases therefore
do not recheck availability; `acquireChess` records the full deficit and later rolls still exclude exhausted stock.
The owner explicitly extended this behavior to already displayed **operator reward** offers; that extension is a
maintainer decision, not footage directly establishing every reward type. Item purchases/rewards remain bounded.
Funds, full-hand checks, per-offer sold flags, merge accounting and selling returns retain their ordinary rules.

Tests: `test/match/community-round30.test.js`, `shared-stock.test.js`, `garrisons_meta.test.js`.
Normative rules: META shared inventory and helper contract; design/match economy.

### 30.17 Official damage numbers and the title-page repository link

Issues #412 and #543. #412's font swap, element-colour pass and bold crit type are not part of this change.
The new setting 「官方伤害数字」 (`officialDamageNumbers`) defaults off. While it is off, every rounded hit and
heal still pops the existing typed number (merge, colours, 90-number ceiling, sparks and slashes unchanged).
Turning 「显示伤害数字」 off still hides every digit, including official ones. The value is sanitised with the
other settings (a missing or non-boolean saved value stays off) and copied into the live field view.

The official rule is PRTS [作战机制](https://prts.wiki/w/作战机制) 「伤害红字（暴击）」 (fetched 2026-10-12): show a
red integer of the final damage when 最终伤害 ≥ 期望伤害 × 1.5, or when a skill forces red. Expected damage follows
the normal formula except that DEF/RES penetration, post-mitigation bonuses (temporary attack-scale increases,
damage-scale increases such as 脆弱, amount rewrites, barrier absorption), final-damage resets and special HP
mechanisms are not counted. Invulnerable targets multiply expected damage by 0, and 0 ≥ 0 × 1.5 still shows red 0.
A dodge of a hit that would have been red shows yellow MISS (`#ff9000`) instead. After 卫戍协议：盟约 the official
client no longer caps red numbers at 10; this client does not restore that cap. The existing 90-number ceiling
stays as a renderer limit [ASSUMED: 「无限制」 means the old cap of 10 is not brought back].

In this sim the comparison runs inside `dealDamage` and does not change HP, shields, hooks or RNG:

- Expected damage is mitigation of `dmg.amount` with penetration forced to 0, times `dmg.mul` only.
- The compared total is the pre-shield amount after source and target multipliers. The integer on the existing
  `dmg` tuple — what both modes display — is what remains after shields and an `hpDamage` rewrite.
- A qualifying tuple that already fires (`amount >= 0.5`, not silent) gains a fifth element `'r'`. Other tuples
  stay four elements. Element gauge fills are not marked. Heals are not marked, so official mode hides them.
- A dodge that already fires sets `num: 'm'` on its fx payload only when the same test says the landed hit would
  be red. That test does not roll RNG again and does not refresh a dirty attacker's stats (a dirty `source.s`
  read rescales HP).

[ASSUMED] gaps, left unmarked rather than guessed:

- `dmg.amount` already includes the attack's stated ATK × 攻击倍率 (`atkScaleMul` is baked in by callers). It is
  not divided back out. `dmg.mul` stays on both sides of the comparison.
- Cached-ATK expected damage (raw ATK, ignoring the attack scale and the target's DEF/RES), forced red, blue
  numbers and yellow BLOCK are not represented on DamageInfo. The three wiki examples are not hard-coded.
- An invulnerable or sleeping hit, and a qualifying total below 0.5, still emit no `dmg` event, so official mode
  cannot show that red 0. Emitting one would increment `events.dmg` and could play an impact. The renderer can
  draw a red 0 when a tuple `['dmg', id, 0, type, 'r']` is supplied. Liftoff and a cancelled or hit-capped attack
  are not red 0.
- PRTS does not say that ordinary hits are silent. That is the requested setting: official mode shows only the
  marked red integer and the yellow MISS. The sentence in the issue comment is not a PRTS sentence.
- The random position is about ±0.35 tiles across and 0.15–0.55 tiles above the head. The simplified board keeps
  its fixed slot and only changes the colour / the MISS label.
- Sparks and the melee slash stay in both modes.
